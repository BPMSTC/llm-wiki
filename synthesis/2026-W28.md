# Synthesis — 2026-W28 (Jul 6–12)

The wiki's bootstrap week: it went from empty to nine interlinked pages. Fittingly, the first thing this LLM-maintained wiki documented was the idea of an LLM-maintained wiki — then it acquired a vocabulary for its own autonomy and a pipeline for feeding itself.

## Themes

- **The pattern documenting the pattern.** Two seed ingests on 07-10 laid the foundation: the [[karpathy-llm-wiki-pattern]] (why LLM-maintained wikis don't decay the way human ones do) and its [[three-layer-architecture]] / [[wiki-maintenance-operations]], plus this repo's own [[capture-workflow]] and [[llm-wiki-repo]] design. The wiki is, from day one, self-describing.
- **Two shapes of autonomous work.** The 07-11 Fable Loop Library capture introduced the week's richest vocabulary: [[agent-loops]] (a cadence — schedule, one change, fixed check, state file, stop) and [[agent-goals]] (a finish line, verified by a judge that only reads the conversation). The [[fable-loop-library]] is 25 worked examples of the loop anatomy.
- **Capture infrastructure that can run unattended.** [[capture-workflow]] recorded a real tooling decision: a dedicated Playwright MCP with a persisted profile replaced driving Brent's live Chrome, which blanked screenshots on any focus change. The [[fable-loop-library]] article (text + 26 images) was the first proof of that pipeline.

## Connections

- The strongest new link is reflexive: [[scheduled-automation]] was re-read through the [[agent-loops]] lens and recognized as a **green-tier loop** — `log.md` is its state file, the schema's commit conventions are its fixed check, and "empty inbox = successful no-op" is its stop rule. The repo's own plumbing turned out to be an instance of the pattern it had just ingested.
- [[agent-goals]]' pasted-proof discipline ("done when the evidence is in the transcript, not when the agent says so") was flagged as directly applicable to verifying unattended runs by their committed output.
- [[karpathy-llm-wiki-pattern]] and [[agent-loops]] were linked as a shared Karpathy lineage rather than two unrelated topics.

## Contradictions & tensions

- **Caution vs. the finish line.** [[scheduled-automation]] commits to a staged rollout — "auto-push enabled only once Brent trusts the output from enough manual runs" — while the whole point of the build is hands-off operation. The gate is prudent, but it means the automation spent W28 built-but-unproven: real behavior under Task Scheduler had not actually been observed end-to-end.

## Open threads

- **Is the unattended run actually verified, or just assumed?** [[agent-goals]] supplies the missing discipline (judge the run by its committed artifact, not its exit claim), but nothing in the loop yet checks its own output. This is the week's biggest unclosed loop — and the most likely place a silent failure would hide.
- **When does the trust gate open?** No concrete criterion was written for "enough manual runs."
- **The capture pipeline has exactly one data point.** Only the [[fable-loop-library]] article has gone through Playwright; the workflow's reliability is one-for-one so far.
